A big update: Apple Music Rich Presence has been rebuilt to be more reliable, lighter on your computer, and nicer to look at on Discord.

## What's new

- **"Listening to Apple Music"**: your profile now says *Listening to* instead of *Playing*, with a progress bar showing how far into the song you are.
- **Album artwork**: your status shows the cover of the song you're playing. When there's no artwork, it shows Apple's current Apple Music logo.
- **Menu bar / tray icon**: see what's playing and whether Discord is connected, and turn the status, progress bar or artwork on and off. You can also set the app to open at login, or quit it from here.
- **Internet radio** shows the song that's playing, not just the station name.
- **New app icon**, made from Apple's current Apple Music logo. On Mac, the menu bar icon matches light and dark mode.
- **More downloads**: one Mac app for both Intel and Apple Silicon, and a new Windows on ARM version.

## Fixes

- Quitting Music or iTunes no longer makes it reopen a second later.
- Songs with " - " in the title (like "Help! - Remastered 2009") keep the right artist.
- Paused songs no longer show a timer counting up.
- The app no longer crashes if Discord isn't open. It connects when Discord starts, and reconnects if Discord restarts.
- **Mac:** the app now asks for permission to read Music, instead of wrongly saying "Music isn't open". It also works when opened straight from your Downloads folder.
- **Windows:** song titles with accents or non-Latin characters are no longer garbled, and no console window flashes up.
- Lighter on your computer: it checks the player once a second instead of twice (every 5 seconds while Music or iTunes is closed), and only updates Discord when something changes.

## Changes

- The "Search on Apple Music" and "Search on Spotify" buttons have been removed.
- Now needs **macOS 12** or later, or **Windows 10** or later. The 32-bit Windows version is no longer available.

## Download

| Computer | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac (Intel or Apple Silicon) | `AppleMusicRPC-darwin-universal.zip` |
| Windows PC | `AppleMusicRPC-win32-x64.zip` |
| Windows on ARM | `AppleMusicRPC-win32-arm64.zip` |

**Mac:** unzip, drag **AppleMusicRPC** into **Applications** and open it. macOS will say it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. When asked whether AppleMusicRPC can control Music, click **Allow**.

**Windows:** unzip and run **AppleMusicRPC.exe**. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info → Run anyway**.

Something not working? Open the app's menu, click **Copy Error Details**, and include that text when you open an issue.
