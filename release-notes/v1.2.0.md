Apple Music for Windows, instant updates, and animated album covers.

## What's new

- **Apple Music for Windows**: works with the Apple Music app from the Microsoft Store, not just iTunes. If both are open, whichever is playing is shown.
- **Animated album covers**: popular albums with a moving cover in Apple Music now show it on Discord too. The covers come from [Animated Album Covers](https://github.com/rustybusgaming/apple-music-animated-covers), which converts the covers of the most-played albums every day. Other albums show their normal cover. Turn it off with **Show Animated Covers** in the menu.
- **Instant updates**: on Mac, your status changes the moment a song changes, plays or pauses, because the app now listens to Music instead of asking it every second.
- **Lighter**: the app no longer starts a new background process every second on Mac or Windows. One long-running process does the work, and it closes when the app does.

## Fixes

- Album art now appears even when looking it up takes more than a moment.
- Changing songs no longer briefly shows the previous song's cover.

## Download

| Computer | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac (Intel or Apple Silicon) | `AppleMusicRPC-darwin-universal.zip` |
| Windows PC | `AppleMusicRPC-win32-x64.zip` |
| Windows on ARM | `AppleMusicRPC-win32-arm64.zip` |

**Mac:** unzip, drag **AppleMusicRPC** into **Applications** and open it. macOS will say it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. When asked whether AppleMusicRPC can control Music, click **Allow**.

**Windows:** unzip and run **AppleMusicRPC.exe**. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info → Run anyway**.

Support for the Apple Music app on Windows is new. If it doesn't pick up your music, open the app's menu, click **Copy Error Details**, and include that text when you open an issue.
