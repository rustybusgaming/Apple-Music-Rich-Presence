Album covers show up more reliably.

## What's fixed

- **Missing covers**: Apple's search sometimes leaves a song out of its results, even when other songs from the same album come up (for example, "Nights" by Frank Ocean). The app then showed the Apple Music logo instead of the cover. Now it uses another song from the same album, which has the same cover, and if none come up, it searches for the album itself.
- **Covers from other countries**: when your computer's country store has no match, the app also checks the US store.
- **Covers after a hiccup**: if looking up a cover failed (a network blip, or Apple limiting how often it can be searched), the cover stayed missing until the song played again. Now the app tries again after 30 seconds, and the cover appears while the song is still playing.
- A song with no match at all is looked up again if it plays an hour later, instead of not until you restart the app.

Songs that aren't in Apple's catalog, like your own imported files, still show the Apple Music logo.

**Mac:** unzip, drag **AppleMusicRPC** into **Applications** (replacing the old one) and open it. macOS will say it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. When asked whether AppleMusicRPC can control Music, click **Allow**.

**Windows:** quit the old version if it's running, unzip, open the **AppleMusicRPC** folder and run **AppleMusicRPC.exe**. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Linux:** quit the old version if it's running, unzip, open the **AppleMusicRPC** folder and run **AppleMusicRPC**.
